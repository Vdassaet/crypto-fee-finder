FAQ

How does ChainRecover AI recover my SOL?

+
Every token account on Solana requires a rent deposit of ~0.002 SOL. When you swap or trade tokens, empty accounts remain open with your SOL locked inside. ChainRecover scans your wallet, finds these empty accounts, and builds a transaction to close them all — returning the locked SOL to your wallet instantly.

Is this safe? Do you need my private key?

+
Absolutely safe. ChainRecover is 100% non-custodial. We NEVER ask for private keys or seed phrases. We only read your public wallet data to find empty accounts. You sign every transaction directly in your own wallet (Phantom, Solflare, etc.) before anything executes.

What wallets are supported?

+
We support Phantom, Solflare, Coinbase Wallet, and MetaMask. For Solana recovery, Phantom and Solflare are recommended. For EVM approval revoking, MetaMask and Coinbase Wallet are supported.

How much does it cost?

+
There is no upfront cost. A 15% service fee is deducted from the recovered SOL. You see the exact breakdown (gross recovery, fee, net receivable) before signing the transaction. You only pay when you successfully recover funds.

How much SOL can I recover?

+
Each empty token account holds ~0.002 SOL as rent. Active traders often have 50-200+ empty accounts. That's 0.1 to 0.4+ SOL sitting idle. ChainRecover scans both SPL Token and Token-2022 accounts to find every last recoverable deposit.
