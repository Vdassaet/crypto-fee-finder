FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything You Need to Know

Is ChainRecover AI safe to use? ↓

Yes, 100% safe. ChainRecover AI is strictly non-custodial. We NEVER ask for private keys or seed phrases. All transaction payloads are generated client-side and sent to your personal wallet (Phantom/MetaMask) for your explicit signature.

How does Solana Rent Recovery work? ↓

On Solana, creating a token storage account (Associated Token Account) requires locking 0.00203928 SOL (~$0.37 USD) as a rent deposit. When your balance reaches zero, the storage account remains open. Closing empty accounts refunds that SOL deposit directly back to your wallet.

What networks are supported? ↓

Phase 1 currently supports Solana, Ethereum, Base, Arbitrum, Optimism, Polygon, and BNB Chain. Bitcoin, Avalanche, Sui, and Aptos support will follow in Phase 2.
